# Stemmie

## How could technology help close the gender gap in STEM?

**Welcome to Stemmie!**
<br>
Stemmie is designed to help people throughout their STEM journey, it is here to provide a place to ask questions free of judgement, connect with people of similar interests and as a resource for learning.

## Features
- **Quiz** - non-technical questions to help young students find career areas of tech they might not know about
- **Inspiration** - motivational quotes hidden in the stars
- **Blog** - posts by industry professionals about their day to day work and how they got there
- **Community** - a forum for people to meet like-minded people and ask questions without judgement from others!
- **Local Events** - events happening in your local area related to STEM
- **Woman of the Week** - highlighting the achievements of a different woman in STEM each week

## Installation
1. Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/BethDrum/Stemmie.git`
2. Navigate to the project directory: `cd Stemmie`
3. Install the required dependencies: `npm install`
4. Run the application: `npm run dev`
5. Open your browser and go to `http://localhost:5173/` to view the application.
