Women of the Week

“Looking back, we were the luckiest people in the world. There was no choice but to be pioneers; no time to be beginners.” — Margaret H. Hamilton

Margaret Hamilton was an American computer scientist. She was best know for leading the software engineering team at MIT, during Nasa's Apollo program. She worked at MIT for over 2 decades and helped forge new paths in computer science and engineering. She actually coined the term Software Engineer!
